The Momentum mobile app has been rebuilt from scratch on the modern Momentum platform. Same account, same sign-in, a faster and cleaner app. It replaces the previous app on iOS and Android, and is available in the App Store and on Google Play.

### ✨ New

- **Today, at a glance:** your next shift and what is happening now, first thing on screen.
- **Sign in with Face ID or fingerprint,** and one-tap Microsoft sign-in where your organisation uses it. Other identity providers are supported through SAML.
- **Offline access:** your schedule stays available without a connection, stored encrypted on the device.
- **Faster push notifications** on iOS and Android.
- **Dark mode,** and five languages: French, English, German, Dutch and Italian.

### 💎 Everything you had, still there

- Day, week, month and agenda views, for you and for your team.
- Give, swap, take, post and bid on shifts, and the job board.
- Time-off and preference requests, with notifications.
- Multi-site accounts and password change.

### 🪲 Improvements since launch (July to September)

- Submitted requests show in your list, and custom hours for requests work on iPhone.
- Assignments without a visit are visible again, and your web filters carry over to the app.
- Android: filter rows respond to taps, and long lists scroll inside their panel.
- iOS: the notifications page opens reliably, and the team schedule search bar is back.
- Week view shows the week number and the role name, and night shifts are no longer cut off.
- Sent requests show the role, the year and the colour code, and can be deleted.
- Preference requests offer periodicity and free date selection again.
- The Call staff button is back, and Face ID is asked less often when you return to the app.
- Editing a preference request no longer fails silently.
- Updates now reach a small group first, then everyone.
