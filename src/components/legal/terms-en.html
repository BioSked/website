“Public Sector Customer” — a Customer that is a contracting authority or other public entity subject to public procurement or public accounting rules.

“Scheduled User” — any individual whose schedule, availability, absences or requests are managed in the Service during a given calendar month. Administrator-only and read-only accounts are not Scheduled Users and are unlimited.

“Service” — the Momentum software-as-a-service platform, including the modules listed in the Order Form, the Mobile App, the AI Assistant (where included), hosting, support and updates.

“Subscription Term” — the Initial Term and each Renewal Term of a subscription under an Order Form; “Subscription Year” means each consecutive 12-month period of the Subscription Term.

“Usage Data” — technical and operational data generated by use of the Service (telemetry, logs, performance, feature-usage and security events), as described in Annex 2, Schedule C.

5. Subscription Metrics, Measurement and True-Up

5.1 Fees are based on the Committed Quantity of Scheduled Users (in pricing bands) and the modules selected. Customer may freely reallocate Scheduled Users among its sites, departments and entities covered by the Order Form.

5.2 Measurement. The Service measures the number of Scheduled Users monthly. The “Measured Quantity” for a month is the rolling average of the three most recent monthly counts, so that short peaks do not trigger adjustments. Measurement reports are available to Customer in the Service and on request.

5.3 Automatic true-up. If the Measured Quantity exceeds the Committed Quantity, the Committed Quantity is increased to the Measured Quantity with effect from the first month of the excess, and BioSked will invoice the additional Scheduled Users (i) at the per-user rate of the applicable pricing band in the Order Form or, where the new quantity enters a band not priced in the Order Form, at the then-current list rate for that band, and (ii) pro rata for the remainder of the current Subscription Year.

5.4 No amendment required. Each Order Form constitutes Customer's authorisation to invoice true-ups under this Section 5 without further signature. BioSked will state the measurement period and counts on or with each true-up invoice. True-ups are invoiced no more frequently than quarterly.

5.5 The Committed Quantity does not decrease during a Subscription Term. Customer may reduce the Committed Quantity with effect from the next renewal by written notice given with its renewal notice period (Section 9.2).

Annex 3: AI Assistant Terms (Momentum Assistant)

A3.1 Scope and capabilities

The AI Assistant is a deliberately narrow, scheduling-scoped conversational interface to Customer's Momentum instance (text on all platforms; optional voice on supported devices). It answers schedule, hours, coverage, absence and request questions and assists with scheduling actions (such as submitting a time-off request, posting or bidding on the job board, proposing replacements, or preparing approvals) — always within the requesting User's own Momentum permissions. It is not a general-purpose chatbot, and it does not provide medical, legal or payroll advice.

A3.2 Activation conditions

BioSked enables the AI Assistant per customer site once the following are in place (and may keep or switch it off where they are not): (a) the Agreement (or the Momentum Assistant Activation Addendum) covering these AI Assistant Terms is in force; (b) Customer has named the administrator/owner of Section 4.1; (c) the in-product user notice and, for voice, the consent flow are live for Customer's Users; and (d) the support and incident path of Annex 1 is operational for Customer. Customer may request deactivation (entirely, per site or voice-only) at any time, effective within 5 business days; per-site activation status is logged.

A3.3 Human oversight (binding design commitment)

Every write action proposed by the AI Assistant (creating or changing assignments, submitting or approving requests, swaps, and similar mutations) requires explicit, per-action confirmation by an authorised User in the interface before execution, with the proposed change displayed before confirmation; approval flows additionally display impact information. The AI Assistant does not execute autonomous changes, does not auto-approve, and cannot exceed the privileges of the requesting User. BioSked will not remove this confirmation design for existing capabilities without treating the change as a Material Adverse Change under Section 20.

A3.4 Transparency and optionality

The assistant is identified as AI in the interface at all times. Users always retain a full non-assistant path: every scheduling task can be performed through the standard Momentum interfaces, and a text-only mode is available where voice is enabled. AI-literacy guidance for Users is provided in the Documentation; Customer is responsible for instructing its Users accordingly (including Article 4 EU AI Act literacy obligations applicable to it as an employer).

A3.5 Voice processing

Voice is optional per User and subject to an in-product consent flow that discloses the speech providers and processing regions before first use, is re-presented upon material provider changes, and can be revoked at any time (reverting to text-only). Audio is processed transiently for transcription and response and is not retained by BioSked; BioSked does not create voiceprints and does not use voice data for biometric identification, emotion recognition or profiling. Medical-vocabulary hints are applied for transcription quality only.

A3.6 Data handling and residency

Prompts and conversation content are processed in memory and are not persisted by BioSked; short-lived conversational context expires automatically. Inputs to the AI Assistant are User-submitted content under Customer's responsibility (Section 4.5). Persisted AI telemetry is limited to the interaction metadata of Annex 2, Schedule C. All AI inference for the assistant (text and voice) runs on the enterprise AI infrastructure of the subprocessors listed in Annex 2, Schedule D — currently Google Cloud (Vertex AI) — hosted in the region applicable to Customer: for EU/EEA customers, assistant processing including AI inference is performed end-to-end in EU regions; for US customers, in US regions. BioSked may substitute AI providers per Section A3.12 and Section 11.4 while maintaining these residency commitments. No Customer Data and no interaction content are used to train foundation models (Section 12.7).

A3.7 Prohibited uses

  • submitting patient data, health records or other data prohibited by Section 4.3(e);

  • using assistant output as the sole basis for employment-related, disciplinary, clinical, payroll or staffing-safety decisions, or representing output as authoritative without verification in the schedule of record;

  • attempting to bypass safety, scope or privilege controls (prompt injection, jailbreaking, automated mass querying, scraping);

  • using the assistant to monitor, profile or evaluate individual employees, or to attempt any inference prohibited by Section A3.8.

A3.8 No sensitive inferences

BioSked does not design, configure or use the AI Assistant or its analytics to infer or deduce an individual's health condition, pregnancy or maternity, parental leave, disability or family status, and Customer will not use the Service for such inferences. Absence-type labels remain plain scheduling data under the safeguards of Annex 2.

A3.9 Regulatory roles (EU AI Act and equivalents)

For the AI Assistant in its current scope, BioSked acts as provider of an AI system subject to transparency obligations (Article 50 EU AI Act), which are implemented in-product (AI disclosure, content provenance where applicable). The AI Assistant in its current scope is not intended for, and must not be used for, the automated allocation of work, performance evaluation or monitoring of workers within the meaning of Annex III(4) of the EU AI Act: proposals become decisions only through human confirmation (Section A3.3). Should BioSked release modules designated as high-risk (e.g., AI-assisted automated planning), those modules will be released with the required conformity documentation and module-specific terms before activation. Customer, as deployer and employer, is responsible for: using the assistant per the Documentation and this Annex; informing Users that an AI system is in use; maintaining human oversight in its processes; completing any required works-council or employee-representative consultation before deployment; and its own lawful basis for workforce data.

A3.10 Output disclaimer

AI output is generated probabilistically from Customer's live Momentum data and may contain errors despite grounding and quality gates. The Momentum schedule of record remains authoritative. Section 16.4 and Section 18 apply; BioSked is not liable for decisions taken without the human verification required by this Annex.
